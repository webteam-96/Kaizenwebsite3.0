Organisation Management System (OMS)

OMS connects sales, HR, finance, projects, and internal messaging into a single system. No more switching between tools. Every decision made on live, accurate data.

Request a Demo All Products

Overview

Most organisations reach a point where their tools stop keeping up. Sales uses one CRM. HR is on a different system. Finance is on spreadsheets. Projects are tracked in WhatsApp groups. Nobody has a single accurate view of the business.

OMS by Kaizen Infotech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. brings every department onto one integrated platform. One login. One source of truth. Complete visibility for managers and team members alike.

What OMS Includes

Sales and CRM

Track every lead, manage your pipeline, assign follow-ups. Sales managers see the full picture in real time.

HRMS

Employee records, attendance, leave management, and payroll – all in one place without spreadsheets.

Project Management

Create projects, assign tasks, set deadlines, track progress. Know the status of every project without a status meeting.

Finance

Invoicing, expense tracking, GST compliance, and financial reporting – updated automatically as transactions happen.

Internal Messaging

Team communication built directly into the platform. No need for a separate chat tool.

Automation

Follow-up reminders, approval workflows, scheduled reports – triggered automatically by rules you set.

Best For

Indian businesses and organisations with 20 to 500 employees running operations across multiple departments – real estate, manufacturing, professional services, and government sector organisations.

How OMS Changes the Way Your Team Works

  • Every department in one view – sales, HR, finance, and projects on a single screen.
  • Managers get real-time status without having to chase updates from individuals.
  • Routine follow-ups, reminders, and reports happen automatically.
  • Full audit trail on every action – know what happened, when, and who did it.
  • New team members onboard faster because everything is in one place.
