Kaizen Events – Event Registration and Management Platform

Online registration, secure payment collection, and QR-based check-in at the gate. Built for events with hundreds to thousands of attendees.

Overview

Running a large event means managing registrations, chasing payments, printing delegate lists, and handling long check-in queues on the day. If any part breaks down, it reflects on your entire event.

Kaizen Events by Kaizen Infotech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. replaces every manual step with a clean digital process. Delegates register online, pay securely, and receive a QR code. At the venue, entry takes under 3 seconds per person. Your team has live attendance numbers the entire time.

How Kaizen Events Works

Online Registration

Branded registration page with custom fields, delegate categories, pricing tiers, and group booking support. Attendees register from any device in minutes.

Secure Payment Gateway

Payments via UPI, cards, and net banking. Confirmed instantly. Registration updated automatically on payment success.

QR Entry Tickets

Every registered attendee gets a unique QR code in their confirmation. The same code is used for entry at the event.

QR Check-In at the Gate

Volunteers scan QR codes on any smartphone. Verification is instant – under 3 seconds. No printed lists, no manual searching.

Live Attendee Dashboard

Organisers see total registrations, category counts, payment status, and live attendance in real time throughout the event.

Automated Confirmations

Every registrant receives an instant confirmation email with their QR code and event details. No manual follow-up needed.

Best For

Membership organisations (Rotary, Lions, JITO), corporate event teams, professional conference organisers, trade associations, and community groups running events with 100 to 10,000+ attendees.

What Improves for Your Event

  • Registrations happen 24/7 without any manual intervention from your team.
  • Long check-in queues are eliminated – scanning takes under 3 seconds per delegate.
  • All payments are digital, transparent, and automatically reconciled.
  • Your organising team has live attendance data throughout the event.
  • Clean attendee data exportable for post-event records, accounting, and future communications.

Kaizen Events – Frequently Asked Questions

Kaizen Events is an event management platform developed by Kaizen Infotech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. that handles online registration, payment collection, and QR-based entry check-in for events of all sizes. It is designed for organisations that want to replace paper-based and manual event processes with a reliable digital system.
When an attendee registers and pays, they receive a confirmation with a unique QR code. At the event venue, a volunteer or staff member scans the QR code on a standard smartphone. The system verifies registration and marks attendance in under 3 seconds. No printed lists or manual checking required.
Kaizen Events supports UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking through an integrated payment gateway. Payments are confirmed instantly and the attendee's registration status is updated automatically.
