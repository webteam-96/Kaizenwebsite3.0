Automated Fare Collection (AFC) System

QR-based fare validation, unified ticketing across metro and ropeway, and real-time footfall tracking. Built for operators who cannot afford downtime.

Overview

Transit operations run on precision. Every passenger must be accounted for, every rupee tracked, and the entry gate must work reliably when thousands of people are moving through during peak hours. A failure at any point affects both the passenger experience and the operator's revenue.

The AFC system by Kaizen Infotech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is built for this environment. Deployed for metro rail and ropeway operations, it provides unified ticketing, instant QR-based validation at entry points, and live operational dashboards – giving operators complete control.

What the AFC System Includes

Unified Ticketing Platform

One system managing fare collection for both metro and ropeway operations. Tickets sold at counters, kiosks, and mobile app – all reconciled in one place.

QR-Based Digital Validation

Passengers scan a QR code at the entry gate for instant, contactless validation. Under 2 seconds per passenger. Designed for peak-hour throughput.

Real-Time Footfall Tracking

Live count of passengers through every gate at every station, updated continuously. Operations managers monitor flow and respond to surges in real time.

Automated Revenue Reports

Daily, weekly, and monthly revenue reports generated automatically. Full audit trail of every transaction. Reconciliation is straightforward.

Multi-Channel Ticket Sales

Counter staff, kiosks, and mobile app sales all captured in the same system. No separate reconciliation needed across channels.

System Monitoring and Alerts

Real-time alerts for gate hardware failures, connectivity issues, or unusual transaction activity. Operations staff notified immediately.

Best For

Metro rail operators, ropeway and cable car operators, and regional transit authorities in India that are replacing manual or legacy cash-based fare collection with a modern, digital system.

What Improves for Transit Operators

  • Passengers move through entry gates faster with instant QR validation – queues are eliminated.
  • Revenue is fully accounted for – every ticket issued and every entry recorded, with no gap.
  • Operations managers have live passenger data to manage platform resources during peak hours.
  • End-of-day reconciliation is automated – no manual counting or cross-checking.
  • The system is built for high-traffic, mission-critical environments where downtime is not an option.
